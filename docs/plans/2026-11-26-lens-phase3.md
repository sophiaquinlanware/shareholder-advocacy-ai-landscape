# Plan: lens system, Phase 3 (Individual investor lens)

Date: 2026-11-26. Branch: `design/lens-phase3` (from `main` at 1bfef97, Phase 2 live). Owner: Sophia Ware. Standing instruction: "Don't ask me any questions about publishing or next steps. Just continue until you complete the project."

## The owner's words (Phase 3 spec)

> "This lens leads with a feed, not the graph. A dense network overwhelms people who aren't specialists.
> - Landing view: an 'Enter your tickers or fund name' input, then a list of upcoming AI-related proposals at those companies, each with a one-sentence plain-language summary (add a plain_summary field to proposals, and fall back to the title if it's missing), the vote date, and a link to the source.
> - Add optional fund-level data: fund nodes with past votes on these proposals (from N-PX filings) and a pass_through_voting flag. Show 'How your fund voted last year' when the data exists.
> - Keep a 'See the bigger picture' button that switches to the graph with their holdings overlay already applied.
> - Write all copy in plain language and avoid jargon. If a term has to appear (exempt solicitation, proxy), give a hover definition."

Earlier owner decision (Phase 1 defaults): "Plain-language definitions appear only in the Individual investor lens."

## What exists (checked 2026-11-26)

- **Upcoming AI votes:** `data/proposals.json` has 4 proposals with `status: "upcoming"`: msft-2026-4, -5 and -6 (meeting 2026-12-08) and csco-2026-5 (2026-12-15). Each has `proxy_url` and `meeting_date`; `upcoming.json` adds the meeting's deadline note.
- **No `plain_summary` exists** on any proposal, so the title is shown until the data session adds summaries.
- **Fund votes:** `data/fund_votes.json` `summary` holds 331 rows of { family, proposal_id, vote, sources, reviewed } for 8 families (Praxis not readable). The rows join to proposals.json ids.
- **Last year's vote:** an upcoming proposal's `vote_history` entries carry `proposal_id` (proposals.json id). The fund's vote on that earlier proposal is the "last year" vote. Example: msft-2026-6 → msft-2025 hotspots.
- **No `pass_through_voting`** field exists; the data session is asked for it (per family, sourced).

## Design

**Where it lives.** It's the Co-filer network tab with the Individual investor lens chosen; there's no new tab and URLs don't change.
- In that lens, a feed section (`#net-feed`) shows first, and the graph area (controls, key, settings, graph and panel) is hidden.
- "See the bigger picture" shows the graph with the holdings overlay applied; "Back to the list" returns.
- The choice is held in `?lens=individual&view=feed|graph`.
- Other lenses are unchanged.

**Input.**
- One labelled field: "Your tickers or fund name", with examples such as "MSFT, AAPL or Vanguard", and a "Show" button. Enter also submits.
- Tickers match company tickers (case-insensitive); fund names match a family name in fund_votes.json (case-insensitive substring).
- What was understood is stated in one persistent live line: "Showing 2 companies (MSFT, AAPL) and the fund family Vanguard. Not found: XYZ."
- Tickers are saved to the same browser-only store as My holdings, so the overlay applies in the graph. The fund name is saved in `localStorage["fund"]`. Both use try/catch, and the line says "Stored in this browser only".

**Feed** (one list, soonest vote first):
- **Each item:**
  - the company name;
  - `plain_summary`, falling back to the title, as the main line;
  - "Vote on December 8, 2026" and the proxy link [proxy statement];
  - the topic;
  - the meeting's own deadline note, quoted as Phase 1 does.
- **"How your fund voted last year"** (only when a fund is entered and the data holds a vote on the earlier version of the same proposal):
  - Text: "Vanguard voted Against the 2025 version (Form N-PX) [filing]".
  - When `reviewed` is false, it adds "matched automatically, not yet reviewed".
  - When there is no row, there's no line; no empty state per item.
- **Empty states:**
  - With no input, all upcoming AI votes are shown (4 today).
  - With input that matches companies with no upcoming AI vote: "No AI proposals on the ballot at Apple. Proposal deadline for the next annual meeting: …" (Phase 2's company deadline, when known).
- **Pass-through voting:** shown once per fund when the data has it ("Vanguard lets some fund investors choose how their shares are voted [source]"). It isn't built until the field exists; the test uses fixture data.

**Plain-language definitions (this lens only).**
- Terms appear as a dotted-underline `<button type="button" class="term" aria-expanded>`. Hover, focus or tap shows a short definition in a tooltip-like box (`role="tooltip"`, linked with `aria-describedby`). Esc closes it.
- Terms: proxy statement, shareholder proposal, exempt solicitation, Form N-PX, annual meeting, co-filer.
- Definitions live in one `GLOSSARY` object in `lenses.js`, with a comment that the owner edits them. They're general descriptions with no thresholds or dates (no regulatory facts in copy).

**Wording guard (Never claim 1–2):**
- The feed lists votes and facts.
- It never says how to vote and never shows `board_rec`.
- A fund's past vote is a filed fact, with its source.

## Data asked of the data session (optional)

- `plain_summary` on proposals: one plain sentence from the proxy's own resolution text, with no judgement.
- `pass_through_voting` per fund family in fund_votes.json: { offered, note, source_url }.

## Revision 2 (after plan review R1: UX 72, accessibility 72, data accuracy 72; content rules failed on fund-vote wording, scope and the definitions rule)

These override the sections above.

**Fund votes in the feed (R1 data 1–3, UX 1)**
- **One join helper** is shared with the Fund votes tab: `votes.js` exposes `window.FundVotes` (`voteText`, `coverageOf`, the summary index, `proposals_checked`, `held_back_proposals`, `not_shown`). It loads before graph.js.
- **The earlier version is only the most recent `vote_history` entry** of the upcoming proposal (`proposal_id`). Its year labels the line:
  - The heading is "How your fund voted last year" when that entry's meeting is within about 12 months before this meeting.
  - Otherwise it is "How your fund voted on the {year} version".
  - Every line adds "(earlier version matched in this dataset by company, topic and similar title)".
- **Line wording**, reusing votes.js: "{Family} funds in these filings ({n} fund records): For / Against / Abstain / Split: 3 for, 1 against", then:
  - the status note "matched automatically, not yet reviewed" (the Fund votes tab's wording) when not reviewed;
  - "from part of this family's filings for that year" when coverage is partial;
  - a link to every URL in `sources`, named "{family} N-PX filing 1, 2…".
- **Rows not shown as votes:**
  - **Needs checking:** NEEDS_CHECK is "Needs checking: votes can't be matched to this proposal with confidence".
  - **Held back:** proposals in `held_back_proposals`, or missing from `proposals_checked`, are never shown.
  - **No row:** shows "No vote found in the filings checked" (full coverage), "Partly checked" or "Not checked: no readable filing covers that meeting".
- **Praxis** (or any family in `not_shown`): typing it shows the `not_shown` sentence.
- **Disclaimer:** quoted once above the list: upcoming.json's `disclaimer` ("This is information, not advice. The site does not suggest how anyone should vote…").

**Matching input (R1 data improvement 1, UX 3)**
- **Parsing:** the input splits on commas and new lines.
- **Tickers:** a token that equals a tracked company ticker (case-insensitive) is a ticker.
- **Fund families:** otherwise a token of 3 or more characters matches a fund family when either contains the other, against family names and their trust names. Only the first matched family is kept, and the live line names it.
- **Not found:** reads "Not found among the 60 tracked companies or the 8 fund families with readable N-PX votes: XYZ".
- Tickers from this field also become My holdings (the hint says so); the fund is kept in `localStorage["fund"]`.

**Feed scope and wording (R1 UX 2)**
- **Heading:** "Upcoming AI proposals at your companies", or "Upcoming AI proposals in this tracker" when no tickers are entered.
- **What's listed:** only proposals with `status: "upcoming"` and `meeting_date` on or after today (US Eastern, rules.js).
- **Empty state:** "No upcoming AI proposals in this tracker for Apple", with Phase 2's company deadline line when known.
- **Date line:** "Annual meeting December 8, 2026" plus the meeting's own deadline note, quoted from upcoming.json ("Vote by 11:59 p.m. Eastern, December 7, 2026."), never computed.

**Plain-language definitions, this lens only (R1 UX 2, a11y 2)**
- **Docs:** PRODUCT.md and DESIGN.md named rule 6 gain the exception, quoting the owner.
- **Pattern:** a disclosure. A `<button class="term" aria-expanded aria-controls>` shows an inline definition right after it, which pushes content down. Esc on the button or the definition closes it and stops propagation. Ids are unique per item. There's no hover-only content.
- **Terms used:**
  - "shareholder proposal" and "annual meeting" in the intro;
  - "proxy statement" next to each item's link, never inside it;
  - "Form N-PX" in the fund line.
- **Glossary:** `GLOSSARY` in lenses.js holds general descriptions with no thresholds or dates.
- **Graph view:** the lens's `sizeLabel` becomes "how many AI proposals each company has had".

**Feed and graph switch (R1 a11y 1 and 3)**
- **Areas:** two siblings under the lens picker. `#net-feed` holds the form, live line, list and "See the bigger picture". `#net-graph-area` holds the tools, holdings, controls, key, settings, graph, list and panel.
- **Feed view:** in the Individual lens, only `#net-feed` shows, and the separate My holdings box is hidden with the graph area.
- **"See the bigger picture"** hides the feed, shows the graph area with the overlay applied, and moves focus to a "Back to the list" button at the top of the graph area. Going back returns focus to "See the bigger picture".
- **Announcements:** both switches announce in `#net-lens-status`, which stays outside both areas.
- **URL:** `view=feed|graph` uses `replaceState`, like `?lens=`.
- **List view:** the Graph/List toggle stays inside the graph area, so List view remains reachable.
- **Drawing:** the graph is drawn or redrawn when its area first becomes visible, so it never lays out at zero width.
- With only a fund entered, "See the bigger picture" opens the graph without an overlay, and the live line says "No tickers entered, so nothing is faded".

**Form and list semantics (a11y improvements)**
- The input sits in `<form role="search">` with a visible label, a hint linked by `aria-describedby`, and a submit button.
- The live line updates on submit only and includes the number of votes listed.
- The feed is a `<ul>`; each `<li>` has an `<h3>` for the company and dates in `<time datetime>`.
- Links carry visually hidden context ("for Microsoft 2026: …").

**Not in Phase 3 (owner's spec change, recorded in STATE.md):** fund nodes in the graph are not added. Fund votes appear as feed lines, because fund_votes.json has no graph edges yet.
