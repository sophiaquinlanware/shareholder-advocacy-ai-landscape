# Plan: lens system, Phase 4 (Policymaker lens) and the year scrubber

Date: 2026-11-26. Branch: `design/lens-phase4` (from `main` at 4602b14, Phase 3 live). Owner: Sophia Ware. Standing instruction: "Don't ask me any questions about publishing or next steps. Just continue until you complete the project."

## The owner's words

> "PHASE 4: POLICYMAKER LENS. The question in this lens is 'Where is voluntary action not working?'
> - Group proposals by what they ask for (use the existing topic tags) and size the clusters by how often an ask recurs across companies and years.
> - Overlay the existing 'Policy or rule' nodes. Mark each recurring ask as 'covered by a rule', 'partially covered' or 'no rule reaches this', using a new coverage field on topic-to-policy edges.
> - In the detail panel, show recurrence count, average support over time, and any company commitments linked through the Causality map."
>
> "CROSS-LENS: … Add a year scrubber under the graph so users can watch the network grow over time. It should work with the existing 'All years' filter, not duplicate it."

## Data (on `main`, from the data session)

- **`data/asks.json`:** 12 asks ({id, label, match}). Topic tags were too coarse, because one topic mixes asks that laws treat differently. The data session chose this finer unit; the owner's "topic tags" are kept as the cluster's parent topic in the panel.
- **`ask_id`:** on 73 of 76 graph proposal nodes. The other 3 have none and aren't clustered.
- **`data/coverage.json`:** 25 entries: {ask_id, policy_id, graph_id, coverage: partial | none, note, source_url, policy_status, reviewed: false}. 23 are partial, 2 none, 0 covered. Every graph_id resolves to a graph policy node.
- **Company commitments:** causality events in lanes `companies` (disclosure, milestone) and `labs` (response), linked to a company by graph "acted in" edges (company → ev- node).

## Design (Policymaker lens only, unless noted)

1. **Ask clusters.**
   - In this lens, graph.js adds one node per ask (kind `ask`, labelled with the ask), linked to its proposals by an "asks for" edge.
   - Proposals sit in a column next to their ask; the ask column replaces nothing else.
   - Ask node size = recurrence (proposals; companies and years stated in text).
   - Proposals without `ask_id` stay unclustered.
   - Asks are synthesized from data files, like Phase 2's gap nodes, and are removed when leaving the lens.
2. **Coverage overlay.** Each coverage entry with a `graph_id` becomes an edge from the ask to the policy node (relation "coverage", with `coverage`, `note`, `policy_status` and `source_url`). Partial edges are dashed and none entries have no edge. The ask's coverage mark uses the owner's three labels:
   - **"Partially covered":** any entry is partial (and none is covered).
   - **"Covered by a rule":** any entry is covered.
   - **"No rule reaches this":** only when every entry for the ask is `none`.
   - **"Coverage not yet assessed":** the ask has no entries. Absence of data is never read as "no rule".

   Every mark and panel line ends with "this site's reading, not yet confirmed by a person" while `reviewed` is false. A `policy_status` of "proposed" or "delayed" is stated as such (Never claim 8).
3. **Marks on ask nodes**, added to the ring table:
   - covered: solid `--g-ring` ring at r+6;
   - partially covered: dashed `--g-ring` ring;
   - no rule reaches this: dotted `--g-risk` ring plus "!";
   - not yet assessed: no ring.

   The mark is also stated in text in the panel, the List view and the keyboard announcement.
4. **Panel for an ask** (heading "This ask"):
   - **Recurrence:** "18 proposals at 6 companies, 2023–2026" (excluding floor proposals).
   - **Support over time:** one line per year, e.g. "2024: 4 votes, 9.71%–32.03%, average 18.6% (average of filed figures, computed by this site)". The owner asked for an average. Every figure used is listed in the List view, links to its results filing, and says when a `support_note` exists. Only proposals with `support_pct` and `results_url` count, and floor proposals are excluded.
   - **Coverage:** one line per coverage entry: "{policy label} ({policy_status}): partially covered. {note} [source] · this site's reading, not yet confirmed by a person".
   - **Company commitments linked through the Causality map:** events in the `companies` or `labs` lane where an "acted in" edge links them to a company that received this ask. Each reads "{company}: {event title} ({date}) [source]". When none: "No company commitments in the Causality map for the companies that received this ask."
5. **Policymaker lens defaults:**
   - layers: asks, proposals, companies/tiers, policy and responses; filers, people and public engagement off;
   - `sizeLabel` "how often an ask recurs (asks); proposals small";
   - the "Same topic" actions heading becomes "This ask" for ask nodes; proposal nodes keep the Phase 1 "Same topic" lines.
6. **Year scrubber (all lenses).**
   - A range input under the graph, "Show the network up to: 2026", from the first to the last proposal year, with a Play/Pause button that steps one year every 1.5 s and stops at the end.
   - It caps proposals (by `year`) and policy and response events (by `date`) at that year, so the network grows; companies, filers and labs stay.
   - It works with the "All years" select: the select still picks a single year, and the scrubber caps. With a year selected, the scrubber's maximum is that year.
   - The value is announced in a live line ("Up to 2024: 31 proposals shown").
   - Play respects reduced motion: steps still happen, but the layout doesn't animate.
   - Kept in the URL as `upto=` (replaceState), and absent at the maximum.

## Revision 2 (after plan review R1: UX 66, accessibility 72, data accuracy 66; content rules failed on commitments, "no rule" scope and the mixed-basis average)

These override the sections above. The source is graph.json throughout (proposal ids, `support_pct` and `results_url` as the network reads them).

**Company actions, not "commitments" (R1 UX 1, data 1).** The data has no commitment field, and the lanes hold refusals and lobbying, so nothing is called a commitment.
- The panel section is "Company actions in the Causality map that mention this ask".
- It lists events at companies that received the ask (graph "acted in" edges from the company, or from a lab the company owns via "parent company"). Each event must contain one of the ask's `match` terms (asks.json) in its title or description.
- Each line reads "{company}: {event title, verbatim} ({kind}, {date}) [source]".
- With no match, the line is "No Causality map events at these companies mention this ask."
- The owner's "commitments" wording is recorded as changed, in STATE.md, until the data marks commitments.

**Coverage wording (R1 UX 2, data 2):**
- **Mapping:** coverage.json values are `full`, `partial` and `none`; `full` maps to covered.
- **Marks:**
  - "Covered by a rule in this map";
  - "Partially covered", plus " (not yet in force)" when every partial entry's `policy_status` isn't "in force";
  - "No rule in this map reaches this" only when every entry is `none`;
  - "Coverage not yet assessed" when the ask has no entries (fixture-tested, since all 12 asks have entries today).
- **Entry lines:**
  - a policy entry: "{policy label} ({policy_status as stored}): {partially covers / covers} this ask. {note} [source]";
  - a `none` entry: its own form, "Checked, no requirement found: {note}", with "source pending" when it has no `source_url`.
- **Review note:** "This site's reading, not yet confirmed by a person" appears once per Coverage section, while any entry has `reviewed: false`.

**Recurrence and support (R1 UX 3, data 2–3):**
- **Floor proposals:** detected by id, `source_url`, label or `support_note` containing "floor", or the title "presented at the meeting" (the votes.js rule). They're counted in recurrence as "1 raised from the floor" but kept out of support.
- **Recurrence line**, computed: "{n} proposals at {m} companies, {first}–{last}: {voted} voted, {upcoming} upcoming, {excluded} excluded, {withdrawn} withdrawn" (zero parts left out). An ask with only floor proposals still gets its node, sized at the minimum.
- **Support by year:** for each year with voted, non-floor proposals that have `support_pct` and `results_url`: "{year}: {k} votes, {low}%–{high}%, average {avg}%". Then, once per section:
  - "Averages are computed by this site from the figures as recorded (most come from 8-K vote counts, not stated percentages); notes are in Proposals";
  - "{j} of these figures are dual-class (Alphabet, Meta), counted by votes cast";
  - "{n} votes without a linked results filing are left out".

  Dual-class means a `support_note` that mentions class or dual. The site rule "no average" (lenses.js comment) gets an exception for this lens, by the owner's spec, recorded in DESIGN.md and PRODUCT.md. No figure in the plan or the tests is typed in by hand; tests read the data.

**Nodes and marks (R1 a11y 1–2):**
- **Ask nodes:** kind `ask`, TYPE_LABEL "Recurring ask", token `--gn-ask` #c7d2fe (11.2:1 on `--g-bg`, measured). In this lens they come first in keyboard order.
- **Coverage marks, at r+9:**
  - covered: solid 2.5px `--g-ring`;
  - partial: long dash `8 3` `--g-ring`;
  - no rule in this map: dotted `1 3` `--g-risk`.

  Each differs from the Upcoming vote ring (r+6, `4 2`) in radius and dash. The panel glyphs are covered ●, partial ◐ and none ○. The ring table in DESIGN.md is updated.

**Year scrubber (R1 a11y 3, UX improvement 1):**
- **Controls:** a visible label, "Show the network up to {year}", and the range input. One Play/Pause button changes its name and uses `aria-pressed`, and keeps focus.
- **Play:** steps one year every 1.5 s from the minimum when started at the maximum, and stops at the maximum. Moving the range, choosing a year or switching lens stops Play.
- **Live line:** one message per step, after the redraw ("Up to 2024: 31 proposals shown"), and "Stopped at 2026" when it stops.
- **Single year selected:** the scrubber is disabled with the text "Showing 2024 only. Choose All years to use the slider."
- **Capping:** proposals cap by `year`, and every `ev-` node (policy, response, public) by `date`. A selected or focused node that the cap hides is cleared, and the panel says so.
- **URL:** `upto=` is clamped to the data's range; a non-number is ignored. A lens switch resets it, as it resets the year. Under reduced motion, each step settles the layout synchronously with no panning.

**Wording:**
- `sizeLabel` is "number of proposals with this ask (asks); proposals drawn small".
- The lens label stays "Policymaker". There's no slogan heading; the owner's question isn't printed as copy.
