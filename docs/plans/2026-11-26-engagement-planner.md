# Plan: engagement planner ("Plan an engagement")

> "do another full sweep of the website ... as if you are an analyst at Parnassus trying to come up with how to engage with an AI company and use collaboration to do so ... completely data-accurate, data-complete, intuitive, dynamic, expertly designed, allows you to brainstorm new ideas to collaborate ... think about what a dream platform would be ... You don't need my approval for anything." (2026-11-26)

## What was built

A study of comparable tools (PRI Collaboration Platform, ICCR guide, Proxy Preview, Climate Action 100+, WBA coalitions, Ceres VWFI, ShareAction, Majority Action, InfluenceMap, FMTI and others) found none that joins proposals, fund votes, coalitions, people, value-chain ties and policy coverage, and none for AI. Its top-ranked feature was a co-filer finder; the planner is that, plus a brainstorming grid.

New section "Plan an engagement" (`#planner`, planner.js), second in the section bar. For one company and one ask, optionally the reader's own organization:

1. **What has been asked where:** asks × US-listed companies; a number is the highest support there, a dot filed but not voted, an outline on the next ballot; arrow keys move; a square opens its plan.
2. **How this ask has gone** (here and elsewhere, with resubmission thresholds) and **timing and rights** (deadline, next ballot, route if Rule 14a-8 ends, threshold, director elections, lab ties).
3. **Ideas:** asks filed at companies with the same role but not here; companies with an open deadline and no filing of this ask.
4. **Organizations with a public record on this ask or company:** lead-filed, co-filed or backed this ask elsewhere; filed or backed here; their funds voted FOR (Form N-PX, with source and review status); holds shares (13F or the holder's own report, with period); shared coalitions. Sorted by kinds of record, then filings. Engagement leads per organization. A shortlist kept in the browser, with CSV export.
5. **Your organization's own record** and **what policy already requires** for the ask, by region.

Every line links its source; wording is scoped to "this dataset"; nothing recommends a vote or a partner.
