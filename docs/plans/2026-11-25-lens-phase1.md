## Scope of Phase 1

1. **Lens control** above the network graph. It's a WAI radio group of three buttons (`role="radiogroup"`, arrow keys). Its state is held in `?lens=impact|individual|policy`, and in `localStorage["lens"]` (try/catch). The URL wins over storage, and the hash (`#network`) is kept. The default is Impact investor.
2. **`lenses.js`**, one file, holds `window.LENSES`. Each entry has `label`, `defaults` (hidden layers, topic, year), `size(node, ctx)` (a multiplier on the base radius), `rings(node, ctx)` (a list of `{ kind, text }`, drawn as rings and always also stated in the panel), and `actions(node, ctx)` (a list of `{ text, href? , button? }`). `graph.js` reads only through these hooks, so a fourth lens is one more entry.
3. **"What you can do" block** at the top of the node panel, fed by the active lens and hidden when empty.
4. **Edge confidence marker**, shown in the panel's connection list and on the lines:
   - **verified**: the edge has a `source_url`; it gets a source link.
   - **reported**: the edge has `confidence: "reported"` or a `source_note` naming a secondary source; it shows "reported" in grey. No edge has this yet.
   - **inferred**: no URL, or `derived`; it shows "inferred from co-filing" in grey and is drawn dashed.

   This replaces "source pending" in graph connection lists only; other tabs keep "source pending" for missing facts. All three markers are grey text; no alarm colours.
5. **Keyboard focus on nodes.**
   - The graph box is one tab stop (`tabindex="0"`, `role="application"`, with an `aria-label` and a visible key hint).
   - Up and Down move through the visible nodes in order of connections, with companies and labs first.
   - Enter opens the node's panel and Escape clears it.
   - The focused node gets a 2px ring and its label shows.
   - The panel is `aria-live`, so the focus change is announced. The List view stays the full text equivalent.

**Wording guard (PRODUCT.md Never claim 1–2):** actions state where and when someone can act (dates, links, contacts, counts), never how to vote and never advice. "Vote at the AGM" names the event without saying which way to vote. Every date and count comes from `data/` with its source link.

## Revision 2 (after plan review R1: UX 72, accessibility 72, data accuracy 66; content rules failed on the wording and on an average)

These override the sections above.

**Action wording: facts, never imperatives** (Never claim 1–2). Each item states where, when or who, from data, with its source link:
- **Upcoming proposal:** "Annual meeting {meeting_label} · {first sentence of the meeting's `note`, verbatim}" plus a proxy link. This is used only when the meeting has `status: "upcoming"` and at least one item, which today means only Microsoft's December 8 meeting. Estimated meetings and meetings with no items produce no action. `board_rec` is never shown in actions.
- **Company:** "Next AI vote: {meeting_label}" plus a proxy link (same rule), "Filers active here: {top 3 by proposals}", and "Open in Company lookup" (a real button).
- **Filer organisation:**
  - "Public profile: {person}" for each person linked by a `works at` edge, up to 3 sorted by name, each linked to their profile.
  - "Exempt solicitations on file: n", counting **distinct** `source_url`s on the organisation's `exempt solicitation` / `exempt solicitation for` / `against` edges. Edges marked "support" are excluded.
  - "Open its filings in Proposals".
- **Policymaker, proposal:**
  - "Same topic: n proposals at m companies, {first year}–{last year}", grouped by `topic`. Free-text subtopics barely recur (48 distinct across 65).
  - "Support across those with a filing: {lowest}%–{highest}% (k votes)". Both ends are values stated in filings; no average is computed.
  - The "linked policy events" action is **dropped**: no proposal-to-policy edges exist.

**Confidence markers** (`rules.js`, new, rule-based config with comments):
- **verified:** the edge has a `source_url` on a domain not in the secondary list.
- **reported:** the `source_url` is on a listed secondary domain (news sites and law blogs, e.g. techcrunch.com, siliconangle.com, bloomberglaw.com, euronews.com, minerva.info, corpgov.law.harvard.edu), **or** the edge's type, description or position contains "UNVERIFIED".
- **inferred:** no `source_url`. Today all 167 of these are `co-filed with`, so the label reads "inferred from co-filing" only for that type, and "inferred" otherwise.
- Marker text uses `--ink-soft` (7.5:1). Inferred lines use a dotted pattern `1 3`, which is distinct from membership links' `3 3`.
- `rules.js` comments say the domain list is this site's judgement and that the owner can edit it.

**Size and intro per lens:** each lens has a `sizeLabel`, and the intro reads "Node size = {sizeLabel}":
- Impact: "number of connections".
- Individual investor: "AI proposals on file (companies)".
- Policymaker: "proposals on the same topic (proposals)".

**Switching lens** applies that lens's defaults: layers, topic and year reset. A live status line says what changed, e.g. "Policymaker view: filers and people hidden. 141 of 294 nodes shown."

**Lens control:** native radio inputs in a `<fieldset>` with the legend "View as", styled as chips that wrap at 390px. Arrow keys work natively. Every change updates `?lens=` and the live line.

**Keyboard:**
- The graph box is one tab stop with `role="application"` and an `aria-label` that names the keys.
- A short live region `#net-kb` announces each Up/Down step: "3 of 42: Microsoft, company, 18 connections, upcoming vote".
- Enter opens the panel; Escape clears it and announces "Selection cleared".
- The focused node gets a solid `--ink` ring with a white gap. The upcoming-vote ring is dashed `--accent`, so the two differ by shape as well as colour.
- Moving focus pans with no animation under reduced motion.

**Text equivalent:** the List view becomes a table of **all visible nodes** with columns Name, Type, Connections, Status (ring text, e.g. "Upcoming vote") and What you can do (action texts). It's sortable by connections, so rings, actions and lens sizing all have a text home. The connections list in the panel shows each edge's confidence marker.

**"Reported" never appears empty:** it shows only on edges that qualify (the secondary-domain list makes about 40 qualify today).

## Not in Phase 1

The open-window aura, seeking asks, resubmission flags, holdings overlay, gap finder, warm path and year scrubber are Phase 2. The Individual investor feed and fund data are Phase 3, and the Policymaker clustering and coverage field are Phase 4. `rules.js` arrives in Phase 2, when the first rule-based value (deadlines, thresholds) appears. No new data fields and no publishing without the owner's go-ahead.

## Build notes (changes from Revision 2, recorded during the build)

- **Dropped: "Open its filings in Proposals" for filers.** The Proposals tab filters by company only; it has no filer filter. A button there would open an unfiltered list, which breaks the no-dead-controls rule. It can come back with a filer filter, which is the owner's call.
- **Added: node labels off by default** (owner, mid-build: "don't label the nodes until you click on a node, and then … label all the connection nodes … the default is to have them off"). Graph settings → Filters → Labels turns them on.
- **Floor proposals are left out of support ranges** (data session: proxy votes could not be cast on them). The data session is setting their `support_pct` to null on `data/value-chain`.
- **Fixed a round-2 bug found while testing:** `app.js` declared a top-level `function history()`, which replaced `window.history`. Tabs had stopped writing their `#hash` to the address bar. It is renamed `voteHistory()`, and there is a new test.
- After result review R1: confidence reads "· source: verified" or "· no source, inferred from co-filing". The List view gets real action buttons and links, plus the lens's measure column (sortable). The Policymaker block is headed "Same topic". Counts are labelled. The meeting note is quoted as "Proxy note: “…”".
- **Added: dark graph canvas** (owner, after R2: "for the graph view i want the black background and colors like this so that the graph looks similar to an Obsidian graph"). Only the graph box is dark; the tokens are in styles.css (`--g-*`, `--gn-*`). Not re-reviewed.
- After R2: exempt solicitation links are named by proposal, or by company and filing year, and listed one per line; the List view shows "–" where the lens measure doesn't apply.
