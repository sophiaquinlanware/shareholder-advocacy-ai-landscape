# Product

What the site is for and what it must never say. Drawn from README.md, SOURCES.md and the site's own footer as of 2026-11-25. The owner (Sophia Ware) changes this file; sessions follow it.

## Users

Stewardship and engagement teams: asset managers, faith-based investors, pension funds, labor funds, foundations and nonprofit filers, plus researchers and journalists who follow AI governance. Mostly on a laptop at work; some on a phone. They want to see the whole field of AI shareholder engagement at once, find each other, and check a fact against its source.

## Purpose

A public, sourced map of investor engagement on AI at the largest AI companies: every AI-related shareholder proposal at the companies tracked, who filed it, who leads engagement at each organization, how public companies tie to the private frontier labs, which routes for investor influence remain as proposal rights narrow, and how policy changes ripple through.

**How it is used (owner, 2026-11-25):** an internal working tool for stewardship teams, not a public showcase. "Make this site like an internal site, so it has a use case for a company … It should be pure using scenarios." The audience is practitioners, so there are no definitions, primers or catchy headings, just "simple data matching". **One exception (owner's lens spec, Phase 3):** the Individual investor lens on the Co-filer network is written in plain language, with short definitions behind a few terms: "Write all copy in plain language and avoid jargon. If a term has to appear (exempt solicitation, proxy), give a hover definition." Tabs follow the tasks: upcoming votes, company lookup, proposals, rights map, co-filer network, contacts, lab pathways, policy and causality, disclosure scorecard, new filings.

**First ten seconds:** the header. "AI shareholder proposals, filers, rights and lab ties", then a meta line of counts ending "every fact links to its source". The owner approved this plain header on 2026-11-25 ("Yes, plain header"), replacing "A shared map of investor engagement on AI" and its lede.

**Look (owner, 2026-11-25):** "largely just black and white, but where necessary, there is blue. For example, all the visuals should be blue." Nothing decorative; a chart appears only if it's drawn from `data/` and updates with it.

## Never claim

1. **No vote recommendations or coordination.** The site informs. Never say or imply how anyone should vote, or organize investors to vote together. The footer line "This site informs. It does not coordinate votes." stays on every page.
2. **No advice.** Nothing is investment, legal or tax advice, and nothing may read as such.
3. **No endorsement or affiliation.** Never imply that any organization on the site (Parnassus Investments, As You Sow, ICCR, PRI, the SEC, any company, filer or fund) endorses, sponsors, partners with or reviewed the site. The disclosure scorecard is scored by this site *against Parnassus Investments' published recommendations*, never "by" or "with" Parnassus.
4. **No unsourced facts.** Every fact links to a primary source. Anything found automatically shows "auto-detected, not yet reviewed" until a person confirms it. Missing sources show "source pending", never a guess.
5. **No invented numbers.** No vote support, holdings, proposal counts or dates beyond what the cited filing says.
6. **People: public professional details only.** Name, organization and role as the organization publishes them. No personal contact details, and no views attributed to a person beyond their own public statements.
7. **No copying the PRI Resolution Database.** Link to it; its terms don't allow republishing.
8. **Proposals are not final rules.** A proposed rule (e.g. the SEC's proposed rescission of Rule 14a-8) is always described as proposed until it is adopted.
9. **No completeness beyond the scope.** "Every proposal" means every one at the companies tracked, from the sources in SOURCES.md, not every one anywhere.
10. **No credentials for the builder.** "Built by Sophia Ware" only; no titles or affiliations added.

## Tone

Plain, neutral, sourced, specific. Example from the site: "It is still a proposal, not a final rule."
