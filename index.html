Sources and scope

Sources. SEC EDGAR filings (proxy statements, vote results, Form N-PX, Form 13F), company and investor publications, and government records. Each row links its source. Records matched automatically are marked until a person reviews them.

Companies. Companies that signed a frontier AI safety commitment, had an AI shareholder proposal on the ballot, were engaged on AI as reported by the investor, or have a compute agreement with a frontier lab. The aim is to model where power sits in the AI value chain and how investors can reach it, not to list every company.

Principles this site follows

  1. Informs; does not coordinate. No vote recommendations; no organizing investors to act together.
  2. Not advice. Not investment, legal or tax advice.
  3. Computed figures labelled. Averages, scenarios and forecasts are marked as this site's.
  4. Public roles only. People appear by name, organization and published title.
