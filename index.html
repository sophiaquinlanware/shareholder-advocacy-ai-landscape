Shareholder Advocacy AI Landscape

A shared map of investor engagement on AI

Investors are pressing the largest AI companies on safety, data center impacts, and what AI means for workers and society. This map shows every AI shareholder proposal, who filed it, who leads engagement at each organization, and which routes for investor influence are still open as proposal rights narrow. It is built so stewardship teams can find each other and see the whole field at once. Every fact links to its source.

This site informs. It does not coordinate votes. Nothing here is a recommendation to vote any particular way, and nothing here is investment, legal or tax advice. Read the company's proxy statement and decide for yourself.

For a full database of shareholder resolutions across topics, see the PRI Resolution Database. This tracker links to it and does not copy from it.

Built by Sophia Ware. Data comes from SEC EDGAR and the public sources linked on each row. Spot an error? Open an issue on GitHub.
